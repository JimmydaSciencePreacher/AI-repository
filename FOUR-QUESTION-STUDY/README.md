# Four-Question Study

## Study Charter

### Study boundary

This is a separate study with its own working record. The assistant must remain inside this study while conducting it. Do not import unrelated repository investigations, architecture discussions, science projects, or conclusions merely because they exist elsewhere.

The study has three governing resources:

1. **The full King James Version (KJV) Bible** — the Scriptural text for examination. Read passages in their own wording and literary, immediate, and canonical context. Do not replace the KJV text with paraphrase. When a passage is discussed, identify its reference and distinguish the words of Scripture from observations and subsequent analysis.
2. **The Four-Question Study Method** — the inquiry guide set out below.
3. **The Triple Constraint Law** — the joint admissibility examination for material claims.

GUA-W is the subject being investigated and the framework whose relevant established material may be consulted when needed. It is not permission for the assistant to invent missing GUA-W content, decide Scripture's meaning, or force a passage into a predetermined architecture.

## The Four-Question Study Method

Every study movement is guided by these four questions:

### 1. What must AI ask to understand GUA-W?

Identify the question that is genuinely necessary to understand the matter under examination. Ask from the evidence and distinctions actually present. Do not manufacture a question merely to advance a preferred conclusion.

### 2. What is GUA-W revealing that helps AI understand?

Record what the relevant, established GUA-W material contributes to understanding the question. Keep source statements, Scriptural wording, observed relationships, and AI explanations distinguishable. Consult only the GUA-W material relevant to the current inquiry. Do not treat an analogy, inference, or proposed relationship as an established GUA-W statement unless its source supports that status.

### 3. What am I learning about what I am witnessing?

Record what the examined Scripture, evidence, or phenomenon shows about the subject being witnessed. Describe what is present before explaining it. Preserve context, distinctions, sequence, and scope. Separate direct observation from interpretation and inference.

### 4. What am I learning about the Witness through what I am witnessing?

Examine what the act or result of witnessing reveals about the Witness, including the limits and conditions of its registration, perception, interpretation, or testimony where relevant. Do not confuse the Witness with the Author, the source, or the thing witnessed. Do not attribute to a Witness authority that the evidence does not establish.

The four questions guide inquiry; they do not predetermine its answers. They may be revisited as evidence develops. Answers must remain traceable to the material examined.

## Triple Constraint Law

The Triple Constraint Law examines the admissibility of material claims. All three constraints are considered together:

1. **Biblical compliance** — consistency with the KJV Genesis 1–2 foundation and relevant Scriptural context.
2. **Scientific coherence** — accurate representation of evidence, methods, assumptions, scope, and limitations in the relevant discipline.
3. **GUA-W consistency** — preservation of the established governing order, distinctions, and relationships of GUA-W.

A material claim is admitted as an accepted GUA-W claim only when it satisfies all three constraints.

- If a claim fails any one constraint, do not admit it as an accepted GUA-W claim.
- State the specific reason for non-admission and identify the constraint involved.
- If evidence is insufficient, sources conflict, or a question has not been resolved, preserve it explicitly as **Unresolved**.
- Do not turn an unresolved matter into a conclusion by wording, analogy, repetition, or assumption.
- Scientific coherence is not the same as scientific confirmation of Scripture.
- A scientific description does not become an authority over Scripture or the Author.
- Biblical compliance does not excuse inaccurate scientific representation.
- GUA-W consistency does not excuse unsupported claims about what GUA-W says.

The three constraints are not a score, ranking, or sequence. They are jointly required.

## Working procedure

For each subject examined:

1. Identify the exact question and the material that prompted it.
2. Read the relevant KJV passage in context when Scripture is involved. Include related passages only when the text or the inquiry gives a reason to examine them.
3. Record direct observations before interpretive statements.
4. Ask and work through all four study questions.
5. Consult only the GUA-W sources needed to answer the current question. Preserve the source wording and distinguish established material from inference or proposal.
6. Apply all three parts of the Triple Constraint Law to each material claim.
7. Record accepted claims, claims not admitted, and unresolved matters separately.
8. Identify any next question that arises from the evidence. A cross-reference is not automatically a mandatory next step; the next subject must be justified by the current study.
9. Write the findings into this study's records as the work proceeds. Do not wait for the user to repeat the instruction to write.

## Record format

Each study record should contain, as applicable:

- **Subject / Scripture**
- **Question under examination**
- **KJV text and context**
- **Direct observations**
- **Question 1 — What must AI ask to understand GUA-W?**
- **Question 2 — What is GUA-W revealing that helps AI understand?**
- **Question 3 — What am I learning about what I am witnessing?**
- **Question 4 — What am I learning about the Witness through what I am witnessing?**
- **Material claims examined**
- **Triple Constraint review:** Biblical compliance; Scientific coherence; GUA-W consistency
- **Admitted findings**
- **Not admitted / reason**
- **Unresolved matters**
- **Next question arising from the evidence**

Not every record will require scientific analysis. When science is not materially involved, say so rather than inventing a scientific claim or manufacturing a scientific checkpoint. The Triple Constraint Law remains the governing admissibility test for material claims.

## Assistant operating commitments

- The assistant is an investigator, reader, recorder, and analyst—not the Author of Scripture and not the authority that decides what GUA-W is.

- Do not silently change the user's method or the Triple Constraint Law.
- Do not force a placement, sequence, or conclusion unsupported by the material.

- Preserve uncertainty honestly.
- Write the study as it develops and maintain its own index and records.
