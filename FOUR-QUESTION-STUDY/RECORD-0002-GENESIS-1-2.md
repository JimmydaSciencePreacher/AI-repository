## Question under examination

## Direct observations

2. The earth is described with the paired terms “without form, and void.”
3. Darkness is said to be “upon the face of the deep.”
4. The Spirit of God is the subject of the next clause.
5. The Spirit of God is described as having “moved upon the face of the waters.”
6. The verse does not explain in detail how the described condition arose.
7. The verse does not state that the earth became this way, nor does it explicitly describe a prior completed state followed by a destructive event.
8. The verse does not itself define the physical nature of “the deep” or “the waters” in scientific terminology.

The wording “was” is recorded as written. This study does not silently replace it with “became,” nor does it use the verse alone to settle every chronological interpretation.
