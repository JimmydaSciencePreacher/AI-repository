# Study Record 0007 — Genesis 1:7 (KJV)

## Subject / Scripture

**Genesis 1:7 (KJV)**

> And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.

## Question under examination

What does Genesis 1:7 record about God making the firmament and dividing the waters into those under the firmament and those above it? How does the statement “and it was so” relate to the command in Genesis 1:6?

## KJV text and immediate context

Genesis 1:6 (KJV):
> And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.

Genesis 1:7 (KJV):
> And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.

Genesis 1:8 (KJV):
> And God called the firmament Heaven. And the evening and the morning were the second day.

Genesis 1:9 (KJV):
> And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.

Verse 6 records the command. Verse 7 records God making the firmament, the division of the waters, and the statement “and it was so.” Verse 8 records God's naming of the firmament Heaven and the second-day designation. Verse 9 begins another command concerning the waters under the heaven and the appearance of dry land. These statements are connected in the account but must not be collapsed into one.

## Direct observations

1. God is the subject of the actions: “And God made” and “[God] divided”.
2. The verse states that God made the firmament.
3. The waters are described in two relational locations: under the firmament and above the firmament.
4. The verse states that God divided the waters under the firmament from the waters above the firmament.
5. The verse concludes: “and it was so.”
6. The wording of verse 7 corresponds to the command in verse 6: a firmament is commanded in the midst of the waters, with the waters divided; verse 7 records the making and the division.
7. Verse 7 does not name the firmament Heaven; that naming is recorded in verse 8.
8. Verse 7 does not describe the material composition, dimensions, or physical mechanism by which the firmament was made or the waters divided.
9. The phrase “and it was so” records that what is described was so; the verse does not separately define this phrase as a general scientific law or provide a technical theory of causation.
10. The text does not state that the firmament independently made itself or that the waters divided themselves. The actions are attributed to God.

The direct observation is the sequence and attribution stated in the KJV. Any further explanation of the physical nature of the firmament, the waters, or the mechanism must be separately investigated and must not be inserted into the verse.
