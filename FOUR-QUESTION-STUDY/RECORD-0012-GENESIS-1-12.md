# Study Record 0012 — Genesis 1:12 (KJV)

## Subject / Scripture

**Genesis 1:12 (KJV)**

> And the earth brought forth grass, and herb yielding seed after his kind, and the tree yielding fruit, whose seed was in itself, after his kind: and God saw that it was good.

## Question under examination

What does Genesis 1:12 establish when the earth brings forth grass, herb yielding seed after his kind, and the tree yielding fruit whose seed was in itself, after his kind, and God sees that it was good? How does the reported result relate to the command in verse 11?

## KJV text and immediate context

Genesis 1:10 (KJV):
> And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.

Genesis 1:11 (KJV):
> And God said, Let the earth bring forth grass, the herb yielding seed, and the fruit tree yielding fruit after his kind, whose seed is in itself, upon the earth: and it was so.

Genesis 1:12 (KJV):
> And the earth brought forth grass, and herb yielding seed after his kind, and the tree yielding fruit, whose seed was in itself, after his kind: and God saw that it was good.

Genesis 1:13 (KJV):
> And the evening and the morning were the third day.

Verse 11 records God's command for the earth to bring forth vegetation. Verse 12 reports that the earth brought forth the vegetation described and records God's assessment that it was good. Verse 13 identifies the evening and morning as the third day.

This record examines verse 12 as the report of what came forth and God's assessment. The command in verse 11 is immediate context and must remain distinct from the report in verse 12.

## Direct observations

1. The sentence begins: “And the earth brought forth”.
2. The earth is identified as the subject of the bringing forth in the report.
3. The verse names grass.
4. It names “herb yielding seed after his kind”.
5. It names “the tree yielding fruit”.
6. The tree is described as having seed “in itself”.
7. The tree and its fruit are described “after his kind”.
8. The verse then records: “and God saw that it was good”.
9. The wording of verse 12 corresponds to the command in verse 11, but the wording is not identical.
10. Verse 11 says “the herb yielding seed” and verse 12 says “herb yielding seed after his kind”.
11. Verse 11 says “the fruit tree yielding fruit after his kind, whose seed is in itself”; verse 12 says “the tree yielding fruit, whose seed was in itself, after his kind”.
12. Verse 11 gives the command; verse 12 reports the earth bringing forth. The repeated subject and corresponding descriptions connect the two verses.
13. The assessment “good” is attributed to God, not to the earth or the Witness.
14. The verse does not describe the physical mechanism, duration, or environmental conditions by which the vegetation came forth.
15. The verse does not define “kind” as a modern biological taxonomic category or provide a complete scientific account of heredity or reproduction.
16. The verse does not state that the earth originated the command. The command is spoken by God in verse 11.
17. Verse 13 records the close of the third day; it is not part of verse 12.

The wording of verse 12 must be read on its own terms. Its additional “after his kind” wording for the herb is present in verse 12 and should not be erased by quoting verse 11. Likewise, the wording about the tree and its seed is preserved as stated in verse 12.
