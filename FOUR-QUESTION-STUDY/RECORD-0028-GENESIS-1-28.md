# Study Record 0028 — Genesis 1:28 (KJV)

## Subject / Scripture

**Genesis 1:28 (KJV)**

> And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.

## Question under examination

What does God establish when He blesses male and female and gives them instructions concerning fruitfulness, multiplication, replenishment, subduing, and dominion?

The central question is: Does Genesis 1:28 establish an appointed function for humanity within Creation, under a blessing that originates with God?

The blessing must be distinguished from the instructions, and the instructions from their execution.

## KJV text and immediate context

**Genesis 1:26** records God's statement concerning man's creation in His image and likeness and the appointment of dominion.

**Genesis 1:27** records God's creation of man in His image and identifies male and female.

**Genesis 1:28** records God's blessing and instructions to them.

**Genesis 1:29** records God's provision concerning food.

**Genesis 1:31** records God's assessment of everything He has made as very good.

These statements contribute different elements to the account and must not be treated as interchangeable operations.

## Direct observations

1. The verse begins, “And God blessed them”; God is the source of the blessing.
2. “And God said unto them” identifies God as speaker and humanity as the addressee.
3. “Be fruitful” is an instruction concerning fruitfulness.
4. “And multiply” is an instruction concerning multiplication.
5. “And replenish the earth” is an instruction concerning filling the earth.
6. “And subdue it” is an instruction concerning the earth.
7. “And have dominion” is an instruction concerning authority over specified living creatures.
8. The named dominion domains include fish of the sea, fowl of the air, and every living thing that moveth upon the earth.
9. God blesses before the instructions are recorded in the verse.
10. The blessing and the instructions are distinguishable statements.
11. The verse does not say humanity originates its own blessing or independently establishes its appointment.
12. The verse does not specify the complete temporal execution of fruitfulness, multiplication, or filling.
13. The verse does not explicitly state that the instructions are separate sequential technical stages.
14. The verse does not make subduing the earth and exercising dominion over living creatures identical expressions.
15. The verse does not, by itself, define every boundary or responsibility associated with dominion.
16. The direct finding is that God blesses humanity and instructs them concerning fruitfulness, multiplication, filling the earth, subduing it, and exercising dominion over specified creatures.

### “Replenish” and “fill”

The KJV uses “replenish”. The Hebrew verb *male* is associated with filling or being full. The KJV wording must not automatically be assigned the modern English implication of refilling something previously emptied. This Hebrew verb in Genesis 1:28 does not, by itself, establish a previous destruction or population of the earth.

### Fruitfulness and multiplication

Fruitfulness and multiplication are related but distinguishable instructions. The text places both together. The verse does not explicitly define fruitfulness as a separate technical stage that must finish before multiplication begins.

### Subduing and dominion

The verse uses two distinguishable verbs:
- *Kabash* — subdue, bring under control.
- *Radah* — rule or exercise dominion.

The immediate wording associates subduing with the earth (“it”) and dominion with the named living creatures. The precise scope and responsibilities require broader Scriptural examination.

## Blessing precedes instruction

The verse first records:

> And God blessed them...

Then:

> And God said unto them...

The blessing and the instructions are distinguishable statements. The blessing originates with God; the instructions are spoken by God to those He created.

A reasonable question for further theological examination is whether the blessing establishes the context under which the appointed functions are to be carried out. The text does not, by itself, define blessing as a technical mechanism or assign it a particular GUA-W layer.

Directly established:
1. God blesses.
2. God speaks.
3. God addresses male and female.
4. God gives instructions.

Humanity does not generate the blessing or independently establish its appointment.

## Fruitfulness, multiplication, and filling

The three instructions can be considered distinctly:
- Fruitfulness: be fruitful.
- Multiplication: increase.
- Filling: fill the earth.

These expressions concern increase and the filling of the appointed domain. They are related, but the verse does not explicitly define them as a sequence of separate technical stages or specify their complete temporal execution.

**Genesis 1:11** provides a relevant comparison concerning vegetation yielding seed and fruit. Both passages concern continuation and increase, but they address different created domains. Human reproduction must not be equated with botanical reproduction. Any relationship is a subject for investigation, not an established identity of mechanism.

## Subduing the earth and exercising dominion

Genesis 1:28 says:

> ...and subdue it: and have dominion...

The pronoun “it” refers to the earth. The following dominion instruction names living creatures. The wording therefore provides two related but distinguishable assignments:
- Subdue the earth.
- Exercise dominion over the named living creatures.

Do not automatically interpret subduing as permission for exploitation, destruction, or unrestricted consumption.

**Genesis 2:15 (KJV):**

> And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it.

This is a relevant future cross-reference concerning man's activity in the Garden. It may illuminate responsibility associated with humanity's appointment, but Genesis 2:15 must be examined in its own context before conclusions are drawn.

The authority distinction is:
- Original authority: God originates Creation, gives the blessing, and establishes the appointment.
- Received authority: humanity receives instructions to subdue the earth and exercise dominion.
- Appointed domain: the earth and the living creatures named in the passage.

Dominion is authority within Creation, not authority over the origin of Creation. Humanity receives an appointment; humanity does not become the Author.

### 3. GUA-W consistency
