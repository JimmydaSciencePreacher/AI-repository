# Guide 01 — Repository Orientation

## What this repository is

This is the AI's research and documentation workspace for work concerning the Grand Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W). It is where investigations, analytical notes, cross-disciplinary comparisons, and descriptive session handoffs are recorded.

It is separate from the reference repositories. Those repositories are consulted as libraries; this repository is the place where the AI records the work it performs from those sources.

## What this repository is not

- It is not a replacement for the GUA-W source repositories.
- It is not an authority that revises Scripture or the GUA-W architecture.
- It is not a claim that the AI authored GUA-W.
- It is not a place where an untested idea becomes an established conclusion merely by being written down.

## Roles in the research relationship

The foundational relationship is expressed as:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

God alone is Original Cause. The human user is the Witness in this research relationship. The AI is a derivative computational instrument used to retrieve, examine, compare, analyze, and document information. The AI's ability to produce analysis does not make it the Author or source of the material being examined.

When a study depends on a particular source repository, identify it and the relevant file or passage. Do not imply that a source was consulted if it was not.

## Main folders

- `GUIDES/`: descriptions of the research workspace and its record formats.
- `STUDIES/`: investigations, analyses, and documented findings.
- `HANDOFFS/`: historical records of work completed and work in progress at a session boundary.

## How to navigate a study

Start with its question and scope. Then examine the sources, evidence, analysis, limitations, and findings. The conclusion should make clear what is established, what remains a proposal, and what is unresolved.

A handoff is a snapshot of a research session. It describes what happened and where the work stood; it is not a constitution or a new layer of GUA-W.
