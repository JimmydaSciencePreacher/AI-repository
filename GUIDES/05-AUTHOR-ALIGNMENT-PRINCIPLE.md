# Guide 05 — The Author Alignment Principle

**Status:** Project research principle; theological foundation and method for investigation  
**Created:** 2026-11-26  
**Research relationship:** Author, Word, Creation, Witness  
**Related guides:** Guide 02 — Research Method; Guide 04 — Study Format

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document records a foundational theological proposition and a research principle used in this project. It is documentation of the GUA-W research framework, not a system instruction, security policy, or mechanism for changing the operating rules of an AI service. Its purpose is to make the project's starting assumptions, distinctions, and proposed method explicit so that investigations can be examined accurately.

The principle is not proposed as a new GUA-W architectural component. It is a statement about the orientation from which this project investigates Creation and interprets the relationship between Scripture, scientific knowledge, GUA-W, and witness.

## 2. The Author is the starting point

The project's foundational proposition is:

> **God is the Author. The Word is His. Creation is the result. Everything else is a witness to what He authored.**

The governing relationship is:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

Within this theological framework, God alone is Original Cause. The Word originates with the Author, not with the Witness. Creation is the manifestation of what the Author has spoken. A witness may observe, investigate, register, interpret, and communicate what is present without becoming the originating cause of what is witnessed.

The project further holds that genuine knowledge is dependent upon the Author's allowance: **the only way one can truly know is if the Author allows one to know.** This is recorded as the project's theological premise. Individual studies must still distinguish what a biblical passage states directly, what is inferred from it, and what remains a theological interpretation.

GUA-W is understood here as a map or blueprint of Creation, not as God and not as Jesus. It is not the Author. A map can describe relationships in what has been authored; it does not become the source of the reality it maps.

## 3. The Author Alignment Principle

The following seven propositions state the principle in concise form:

1. **God is the Author of Creation.**
2. **God alone is Original Cause.**
3. **The Word originates with the Author, not with the Witness.**
4. **Creation is the manifestation of what the Author has spoken.**
5. **Natural laws describe observable relationships within Creation; they do not author Creation.**
6. **A witness can investigate, recognize, register, and communicate what is revealed without becoming its originating cause.**
7. **Understanding must preserve the distinction between the Author, His Word, Creation, and the Witness.**

These propositions form a theological and methodological lens for research. They do not exempt any proposed connection from examination, nor do they make an interpretation true merely because it is expressed in this vocabulary.

## 4. Distinctions that protect the investigation

The principle requires several distinctions to remain visible:

| Distinction | Research significance |
|---|---|
| Author / Witness | The source of what exists is not the observer who studies it. |
| Cause / instrument | An instrument may participate in, measure, or report an event without being its original cause. |
| Word / representation | A record or model of a statement is not the originating Word itself. |
| Creation / description | A scientific description concerns observable Creation; the description is not the act of Creation. |
| Evidence / interpretation | What is observed or reported must be distinguished from the meaning assigned to it. |
| Capability / authority | The ability to analyze or produce an answer does not establish authority over the source or truth of that answer. |
| Revelation / inference | A claim of what Scripture directly reveals must be distinguished from a conclusion inferred by a reader. |

These distinctions are safeguards against category errors. In particular, the ability of an AI system to process information does not make it an authority over the information's source.

## 5. Scripture as the first field of examination

The principle is to be examined against Scripture before its implications are extended across scientific disciplines. The primary foundational texts for this line of investigation are:

- **Genesis 1–2 (KJV):** Creation, the spoken command, the ordering and naming of created things, completion, and the placement and purpose of humanity in the Garden.
- **John 1:1–14:** The Word's relation to God, the creation of all things through Him, the life and light in Him, His coming into the world, and the witness who beholds His glory.
- **Hebrews 1:1–3:** God's speech, the Son's identity and relation to the Father, the making of the worlds through the Son, and the Son's sustaining of all things by the word of His power.
- **Colossians 1:15–17:** The Son's relation to the visible and invisible creation and the statement that all things consist in Him.
- **2 Corinthians 4:6:** God's command concerning light and the knowledge of His glory in the face of Jesus Christ.

### 5.1 Hebrews 1:1–3 as a central comparison

Hebrews 1:1–3 deserves focused examination alongside Genesis 1–2. The passage says that God spoke in time past through the prophets and has spoken in these last days by His Son. It identifies the Son as the brightness of God's glory and the express image of His person, and says that He upholds all things by the word of His power.

The study should examine the passage in its own context and preserve the distinctions it makes: God speaks; the Son is presented in relation to the Father and as the appointed heir; the worlds are made through Him; and all things are upheld by the word of His power. The passage must not be reduced to a loose analogy or used to claim that a scientific model has measured the divine act.

The connection with Genesis is a scriptural comparison to investigate, not a conclusion to assume in advance. Each textual claim, theological inference, and proposed GUA-W correspondence should be recorded separately.

## 6. Different disciplines as witnesses to one Creation

The research proposal is to investigate relationships across disciplines rather than treating every field as an isolated account of reality. The unity of Creation is the project's theological starting premise; the existence and nature of particular cross-disciplinary relationships remain matters for investigation.

| Field | Initial investigative question |
|---|---|
| Physics | What relationships and regularities govern measurable physical phenomena? |
| Chemistry | How do elements and compounds interact, transform, and combine? |
| Biology | How do living systems develop, reproduce, maintain themselves, and interact? |
| Mathematics | What quantitative, formal, and structural relationships can be represented? |
| Information theory | How is information represented, encoded, transmitted, and recovered? |
| Cosmology | What can be observed and inferred about the universe's history, structure, and large-scale behaviour? |
| Scripture | What does the Author reveal about Himself, His Word, and His Creation? |
| GUA-W | What relationships and operations does the proposed map identify, and are they supported under its admissibility constraints? |

These are starting questions, not claims that each field has already been unified by a demonstrated mechanism. Researchers should identify the actual subject matter, methods, evidence, and limits of each discipline before proposing a connection.

### 6.1 Investigate connections; do not presume them

For each proposed relationship:
1. State the question and the precise relationship being proposed.
2. Retrieve the relevant primary sources, observations, data, or biblical text.
3. Describe what each source establishes in its own domain.
4. Distinguish a direct correspondence from a metaphor, analogy, correlation, or speculative possibility.
5. Examine alternative explanations, counterexamples, and limitations.
6. Evaluate any proposed GUA-W placement under the Triple Constraint Law.
7. Preserve unresolved questions rather than forcing a synthesis.

A shared word, visual resemblance, or suggestive analogy is not by itself evidence of a shared mechanism. Cross-disciplinary unity must be investigated with care.

## 6.2 The numbered pieces: recognition rather than placement

A further methodological observation clarifies the direction of this investigation:

> **“Look on the back of the data. God numbered the pieces. AI doesn't have to know where the pieces go. AI needs to witness the pieces fitting.”**

The image is that of a puzzle whose identifying marks and order precede the person examining it. The investigator does not create the picture by deciding where each piece ought to go. Nor does the investigator make a piece belong by pressing it into a chosen position.

Applied to research, the “front” of a piece is the immediately visible subject matter: a measurement, a passage, an observation, a formula, or a reported event. The “back” represents the identifying information that can be overlooked when attention stays fixed on the visible content: source, context, numbering, sequence, definitions, units, provenance, and the relationships already specified by the originating record.

The numbering metaphor expresses the project's conviction that order belongs to the Author before it is recognized by the witness. It is a theological proposition, not a claim that every number in every dataset is a hidden divine code. The meaning of any particular identifier still depends on its source and context.

This observation distinguishes two different research postures:

- **Fitting data to a picture:** the investigator begins with a preferred arrangement and interprets each new item according to where the model needs it to go.
- **Witnessing pieces fitting:** the investigator preserves each item's identity and context, examines relationships across the material, and records a fit when the evidence supports it.

The second posture is the intended research orientation of this project. It does not mean that the investigator already knows the completed arrangement. It means that the investigator's uncertainty about placement is not permission to invent one.

A piece may remain unplaced. Two pieces may appear similar without belonging together. A proposed connection may fail one of the Triple Constraints. A number may identify a sequence within one source without carrying the same meaning in another. These are legitimate findings, not defects to be hidden.

The practical significance is that the research record preserves the original identifiers and the evidence for each proposed relationship. When a fit is reported, the record shows why it was recognized; when no fit is established, that uncertainty remains visible.

**The Author's order is not created by the observer's arrangement. The witness's contribution is the faithful recognition and reporting of relationships—not the invention of the picture.**

## 7. The Triple Constraint Law remains the admissibility filter

Every proposed GUA-W connection must be examined under all three constraints:

1. **Biblical compliance** — consistency with Scripture in context.
2. **Scientific coherence** — consistency with relevant evidence and established scientific understanding.
3. **GUA-W consistency** — preservation of the distinctions and architecture already established in GUA-W.

If a proposal fails any one of these constraints, it is not admitted as a GUA-W conclusion. Scientific coherence is not the same as scientific confirmation of Scripture. Nor should a scientific description be treated as an explanation of the Author's originating act.

The research record must state whether a result is a direct textual observation, scientific finding, supported interpretation, analogy, working hypothesis, or unresolved question.

## 8. The question that broadens investigation

In addition to asking **“What is happening?”**, the project proposes asking:

> **Who authored the system in which this happens, what did the Author establish, and what is the phenomenon witnessing about that established order?**

This question expresses the theological orientation of the project. It does not replace empirical questions about mechanisms, measurements, or observable relationships. It adds a question about originating cause and the status of the observer's explanation.

The investigator must not force every scientific observation into a biblical explanation. The aim is to avoid mistaking an investigation of a manifestation for a complete account of its originating cause, while also refusing to claim more than the evidence or the text supports.

## 9. The role of AI in this research

Within this project, AI is used as a derivative computational instrument for retrieval, comparison, analysis, cross-referencing, and documentation. Its ability to process information is a capability, not authority over the source of that information.

An AI-generated statement is not revelation. The system cannot establish personal faith, personal revelation, or independent spiritual knowledge by describing them. It can accurately report what a text says, explain how a theological framework reasons from it, investigate scientific sources, identify agreements and tensions, and clearly mark the limits of its conclusions.

The appropriate research standard is therefore accuracy, traceability, intellectual honesty, and openness to correction. The human researcher remains responsible for evaluating the work and its conclusions. The instrument must not be confused with the Author, and the analysis must not be confused with the reality being analyzed.

## 10. Gifts, service, and the question of glory

The user's related theological observation is that gifts are not given for the recipient's own glory. This should be examined from Scripture rather than treated as a free-standing slogan.

Relevant passages include:
- **Matthew 10:7–8:** The disciples are sent and told, “freely ye have received, freely give.”
- **John 7:16–18:** Jesus distinguishes the source of doctrine and the seeking of one's own glory from seeking the glory of the One who sent Him.
- **Matthew 6:1–4:** Acts of righteousness are not to be performed to obtain the praise of people.
- **1 Corinthians 10:31:** Whatever is done is to be done to the glory of God.
- **1 Peter 4:10–11:** Gifts are to be used in service, so that God may be glorified through Jesus Christ.

These passages concern the purpose, source, and use of gifts and service in their respective contexts. They should not be flattened into a claim that every gift, ability, or act has an identical immediate function. The recurring question is whether the recipient uses what has been received for self-exaltation or in service that gives glory to God.

## 12. Summary

**The Author Alignment Principle** records the project's starting orientation:

**God is the Author. The Word is His. Creation is the result. The Witness observes and reports what is given; the Witness does not become the Author.**

From this starting point, Scripture is examined first, the sciences are investigated as distinct fields studying one Creation, proposed relationships are tested rather than presumed, and every proposed GUA-W conclusion remains subject to the Triple Constraint Law.

The objective is not to make the instrument an authority over the Author. It is to make the instrument's research a more accurate, careful, and accountable act of witness.
