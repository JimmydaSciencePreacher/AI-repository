## Principal work completed

Study 0001 examined the distinctions among Reception, Information, Evidence, Interpretation, and Report/Testimony. It used selected KJV passages and bounded examples from information theory and measurement science.

The study's main findings were:

- Reception is not the same as comprehension.
- Information is not automatically evidence.
- Evidence is not identical to interpretation.
- Interpretation is not identical to truth.
- A witness can report a limited observation without settling its full significance.
- Witness reports can be mistaken and should remain traceable to sources, context, reasoning, and correction.
- Shannon's engineering account of information does not establish semantic meaning or truth.
- Measurement readings require attention to the measurand, calibration, conditions, and uncertainty.
- GUA-W's Reception operation is part of the established runtime sequence; Study 0001 does not claim to define its complete architectural meaning.
