## Study 0005 central findings

- Correlation alone does not establish causal direction or structure.
- A common cause can produce an association between two variables.
- Temporal precedence alone does not prove causation.
- Mechanistic evidence and interventions can strengthen causal explanations, subject to their assumptions and limitations.
- Observational causal inference is possible but requires careful methods.
- Not every explanation is causal; mathematical and structural explanations also exist.
- Proximate physical cause and ultimate theological source are distinct questions.
- The GUA-W theological claim of Original Cause does not replace empirical investigation of physical processes; scientific causal models do not, by themselves, identify or replace the Author.
- No GUA-W architecture change is proposed.
