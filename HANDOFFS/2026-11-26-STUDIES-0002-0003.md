### Study 0003 — Attention, Expectation, Memory, and Mistaken Witness

Created:

- `STUDIES/0003-ATTENTION-EXPECTATION-MEMORY-MISTAKEN-WITNESS.md`

The study examines:
- attention and selective encoding;
- expectation and memory bias;
- memory as encoding, retention, and retrieval rather than a perfect recording;
- source monitoring and source misattribution;
- false recognition and memory distortion;
- the distinction between sincere recollection and accurate recollection;
- implications for AI research provenance and source checking.

KJV passages examined include Deuteronomy 19:15–21; Proverbs 14:15, 18:13, 17; Exodus 20:16; John 8:13–18; Luke 1:1–4; and 1 John 1:1–3.

Scientific literature includes work by Johnson, Hashtroudi, and Lindsay on source monitoring; Mitchell and Johnson's review; Schacter and Slotnick on memory distortion; Bein and colleagues on prediction and memory; and Schacter on memory and technology.
