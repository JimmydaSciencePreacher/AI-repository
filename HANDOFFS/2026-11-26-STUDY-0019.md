## Central question

Why does Ezekiel 37 describe bones coming together and bodies receiving sinews, flesh, and skin before breath enters them? What does the passage establish about formation, breath, life, and God's Spirit?

## Principal findings

1. Ezekiel 37:1–14 presents a vision initiated by the LORD.
2. The LORD commands Ezekiel to prophesy to the bones.
3. The bones come together; sinews, flesh, and skin appear.
4. Verse 8 explicitly states that there is no breath in them.
5. The LORD then commands Ezekiel to prophesy to the breath/wind.
6. Breath enters; they live and stand as an exceeding great army.
7. The Hebrew word *ruach* is used in the chapter in contexts rendered spirit, wind, and breath.
8. Verse 11 explicitly identifies the bones as the whole house of Israel.
9. Verses 12–14 interpret the vision as the LORD's promise to open graves, restore His people, put His spirit in them, and place them in their land.
10. Ezekiel is the commissioned witness and speaker, not the source of the word or the giver of life.
11. Genesis 2:7 uses *neshamah*, whereas Ezekiel 37 uses *ruach*; the passages are comparable but their wording is not identical.
12. The vision distinguishes formed bodies from living bodies, but its explicit interpretation is Israel's restoration.
13. No GUA-W architecture change is proposed.

## Important distinctions preserved

- The vision's order is bones, sinews, flesh, skin, absence of breath, arrival of breath, life, and standing.
- The first prophetic command is addressed to the bones; the second is addressed to the breath/wind.
- The LORD's own interpretation in verses 11–14 governs the meaning: the bones are the whole house of Israel.
- The vision is not primarily an anatomy lesson, laboratory demonstration, or general biological sequence.
- *Ruach* can be rendered spirit, wind, or breath according to context; the English renderings do not make every occurrence identical in meaning.
- Genesis 2:7 uses *neshamah*, not *ruach*.
- Ezekiel is commissioned to speak; the LORD remains the source of the message and restoration.
- The passage does not establish that structure alone produces life or that this sequence is a universal GUA-W law.
