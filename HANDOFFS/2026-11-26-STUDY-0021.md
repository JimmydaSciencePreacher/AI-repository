## Central question

What does Scripture teach about God's work in a person in relation to willing and doing?

## Principal findings

1. Philippians 2:12 gives believers a real command to work out their salvation with fear and trembling.
2. Philippians 2:13 grounds that command in God's work within believers, explicitly including both willing and doing.
3. The immediate context concerns Christlike humility, unity, obedience, and the conduct of the church.
4. Ezekiel 36:26–27 attributes the new heart, new spirit, indwelling of God's Spirit, and resulting obedience to God's action.
5. John 6:37–45 describes real coming to Christ while identifying the Father's giving and drawing as necessary.
6. Romans 8:1–17 describes the Spirit's indwelling and believers' action through the Spirit.
7. Romans 9:16 explicitly denies that God's mercy originates in human willing or running.
8. These passages preserve both divine initiative and real human response, without presenting them as equal original causes.
9. The statement that God's work is foundational and human willing and doing are real responses is a canonical synthesis, not a verbatim statement from one verse.
10. The passages have distinct contexts and should not be flattened into a single undifferentiated formula.
11. No GUA-W architecture change is proposed.

## Important distinctions preserved

- Philippians 2:12's command and verse 13's explanation must be read together.
- “Work out” must not be turned into a claim that humans originate or purchase salvation.
- God's work in believers explicitly includes willing and doing.
- Human coming, obeying, and acting remain real biblical responses.
- John 6:44 identifies the Father's drawing as necessary for coming to Christ.
- Romans 8:13 describes believers putting deeds of the body to death through the Spirit.
- Romans 9:16 denies that God's mercy originates in human willing or running.
- Divine initiative and human response must not be treated as equal original causes or as mutually exclusive.
- The canonical synthesis is not a direct quotation from one verse and must retain each passage's context.
- No new GUA-W layer, runtime operation, state, or formal definition is proposed.
