## Research question

What does Jesus mean by “greater works” in John 14:12, and how does this promise connect to His going to the Father, the Holy Ghost, prayer in His name, abiding in Christ, and the expanding witness in Acts?

## Principal findings

1. John 14:10 says the Father dwelling in the Son does the works; John 14:11 presents the works as witness to Jesus.
2. John 14:12 promises that believers will do Jesus' works and greater works because He goes to the Father.
3. The Johannine term “works” is broader than miraculous signs alone, although signs remain part of the immediate context.
4. “Greater” does not mean that believers become greater than Christ in identity, rank, or authority.
5. Interpretations include greater geographical reach, redemptive-historical/qualitative work, continuation of miraculous signs, and an integrated reading.
6. The most comprehensive reading recorded is that the risen Christ's mission enters a greater phase of reach and Spirit-enabled witness after His going to the Father. The gospel spreads to the nations; the Spirit gives life and bears witness; signs may accompany the mission but do not exhaust its meaning.
7. John 14:13–14 connects prayer in Jesus' name with the Father's glory in the Son. The name is not a formula for compelling outcomes.
8. John 14:15–17, 26; 15:26–27; and 16:7–15 connect Jesus' departure with the Comforter's coming, the Spirit's testimony, and Christ's glorification.
9. John 15:1–8 says fruit is borne by abiding in Christ; the branch does not generate life independently.
10. Acts 1:8, 2:33, and subsequent mission narratives portray the Spirit-empowered expansion of witness after Jesus' ascension.
11. 1 Corinthians 12 does not say every believer has every gift.
12. No GUA-W architecture change is proposed.
