## Scope and principal content

The study:
- Records the user's proposal as a working hypothesis, not as an already-established scientific result.
- Examines the KJV sequence in Genesis 1:3–5: God speaks; light exists; God sees and evaluates; God separates; God names; evening and morning are identified as the first day.
- Includes selected Hebrew forms as language aids, while cautioning against treating glosses as a complete theology.
- Preserves the important distinction between the light of day one and the luminaries introduced in Genesis 1:14–18.
- Describes modern physical light as electromagnetic radiation, with visible light a limited portion of the electromagnetic spectrum, and distinguishes physical measurement from ultimate theological explanation.
- Treats natural laws as descriptions of physical regularities within Creation, not as authorities over the Creator.
- Proposes a provisional correspondence table with existing GUA-W terms but explicitly makes no layer, operation, state, or definition changes.
- Clarifies that AI can retrieve, compare, and report evidence as an investigative instrument; it is not the Author, originating Word, Holy Ghost, spiritual authority, or source of revelation.

## Principal findings

1. Genesis 1:3 explicitly records God's speech and the resulting existence of light.
2. Genesis 1:4–5 records divine seeing/evaluation, separation, naming, and the first day.
3. Genesis introduces luminaries in Genesis 1:14–18, after the light of day one; the physical relation between these descriptions remains unresolved in this study.
4. Modern physics measures and models electromagnetic radiation, including visible light, wavelength, frequency, propagation, and interactions with matter.
5. Such measurements describe physical phenomena; they do not independently identify an ultimate theological cause.
6. The theological interpretation that God's command is the originating act and light is the created result is grounded in the narrative sequence. It is not a scientific conclusion.
7. GUA-W can be used provisionally to map relationships between source, command, created result, distinction, designation, measurement, and witness, provided no one-to-one architecture mapping is forced.
8. The claim that modern physics has identified exactly what Genesis 1:3 means physically is not established.
9. No GUA-W architecture change was made.

## Triple Constraint disposition

The textual sequence and the physical description of measurable light can be compared as distinct domains. The stronger identification of Genesis 1:3 light with a specific modern physical model remains unestablished. Scientific coherence is not scientific confirmation of Scripture.
