Title: **Study 0047 — John 3:3–8: Born from Above and Born of the Spirit**

Creation commit: `327b7a264cb3921c5b3f17726cb63353ab3cd744`

The study examines John 3:1–15, especially verses 3–8. It considers the double sense of Greek *anōthen* (“again/from above”), the distinction between birth of flesh and birth of Spirit, the phrase “water and the Spirit” in relation to Ezekiel 36:25–27, and the wind/Spirit wordplay in John 3:8.

## Principal findings recorded

1. Jesus says birth from above is necessary to see and enter the kingdom of God.
2. Natural birth and birth from the Spirit are distinct; flesh gives birth to flesh, and the Spirit gives birth to spirit.
3. Ezekiel 36:25–27 is a strong Old Testament background for cleansing, a new heart, a new spirit, and God's Spirit within His people.
4. The precise interpretation of “water” in John 3:5 remains debated and is recorded as an interpretive question.
5. In John 3:8, *pneuma* carries the wind/Spirit wordplay. The audible wind is an observable effect; it is not the source and does not control the Spirit.
6. The wind comparison is an analogy for the observer's limits, not a physical mechanism of regeneration.
7. No GUA-W architecture change is proposed.
