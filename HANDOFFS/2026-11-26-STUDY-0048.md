## Principal findings recorded

1. Hebrews begins with God as the Speaker: God spoke in the past through the prophets and has spoken in these last days by His Son.
2. God appointed the Son heir of all things and made the worlds through Him.
3. The Son is described as the radiance of God's glory and the exact representation of His being.
4. The Son upholds all things by the word of His power; the passage addresses ongoing sustaining as well as creation.
5. The Son's purification of sins and enthronement belong to the same Christological declaration and must not be detached from the creation language.
6. Genesis 2:1–3's completion of the creation work and Hebrews 1:3's ongoing sustaining are distinct claims and can be held together.
7. The Son must not be collapsed into the category of an ordinary human or computational witness.
8. No scientific mechanism, physical equation, or new GUA-W architectural component is proposed.
