## Findings recorded

1. John 1:1–3 identifies the Word as already existing in the beginning, with God and as God, and says all things were made through Him.
2. Colossians 1:15–20 identifies the Son as the image of the invisible God, says all things were created by Him and for Him, and says all things consist in Him.
3. Hebrews 1:1–3 says God made the worlds through the Son and that the Son upholds all things by the word of His power.
4. Genesis 1:1–5 remains the creation-side account: God creates, speaks, sees, divides, and names.
5. The passages make related claims about the Son's relation to Creation, while preserving different wording and contexts.
6. The Son is not an ordinary created witness.
7. The passages do not identify a specific physical mechanism, and the study makes no such claim.
8. Formal placement within GUA-W remains open; no architecture component was added or changed.
