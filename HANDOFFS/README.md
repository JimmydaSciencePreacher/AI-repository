# Handoffs

This folder contains descriptive records of research sessions and transitions between sessions.

A handoff may record:

- the questions examined;
- the sources and repository files actually consulted;
- studies or other files created or changed;
- findings and corrections made during the session;
- unresolved questions and limitations;
- the state of the work at the time the handoff was written.

A handoff is a historical snapshot. It describes what happened; it does not establish new GUA-W authority, create an AI constitution, or prescribe conclusions for future research.

Use dates or sequential identifiers in filenames so that records can be located and understood in chronological context.
