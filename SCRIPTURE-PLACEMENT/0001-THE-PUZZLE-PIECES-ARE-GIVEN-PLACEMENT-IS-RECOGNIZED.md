## 1. Question

Can Scripture be examined verse by verse as given material, with each verse identified and placed only where the already-completed GUA-W says it belongs, allowing every supported cross-placement to be recorded without moving or forcing a verse?

## 2. Governing instruction received

The researcher has established this method:

> Scriptures are the puzzle pieces. AI did not write them and is not the one who decides their meaning. AI can number them and fit them together only where they fit according to where GUA-W says they get placed. There are a finite number of verses; the work continues until the verses have been accounted for. If a Scripture cross-fits into many places, record those places. The verses do not move from where GUA-W places them.

This is recorded as the user's governing instruction for this separate study. AI's task is retrieval, identification, contextual examination, comparison with the authoritative GUA-W records, cross-referencing, and faithful documentation. AI is not the Author of Scripture and does not become the authority that assigns Scripture its meaning.

## 3. Scope

The initial corpus is the KJV Bible, examined in canonical order, beginning at Genesis 1:1. Each verse retains its canonical identifier. Contextual ranges will be examined wherever needed to avoid fragmenting the passage.

The objective is comprehensive accounting of the defined corpus. A verse may have one placement, several supported placements, or remain unresolved while further source examination is undertaken. Unresolved does not mean excluded.

This series is deliberately separate from the repository's existing Studies 0001–0050. It has its own index, numbering, progress record, and handoffs. It does not replace or renumber any previous study.

## 5. Analysis: what “number and place” means

**Numbering** means retaining the verse's actual canonical reference and recording its position in the examination sequence. AI does not create a new numbering system that changes the text.

**Placement** means identifying a relationship that the authoritative GUA-W records themselves establish. A verse's wording and context must support the comparison. The GUA-W source citation must be recorded beside the placement so the reader can inspect the basis.

**Multiple placement** means one verse may witness to more than one established GUA-W place. Each placement is separately documented. A second supported placement does not cancel, displace, or rewrite the first.

**No forced fit** means that resemblance of vocabulary, a convenient analogy, or an AI-generated interpretation is insufficient. When the source records do not yet establish a precise placement, record the verse as unresolved and investigate further.

**No silent movement** means a verse is never transferred away from a placement already identified by the authoritative GUA-W record. If an apparent conflict is found, document the source references and the conflict rather than silently changing the placement.

## 6. Placement record

Each examined verse will be tracked using these fields:

| Field | Required record |
|---|---|
| Canonical identifier | Book, chapter, verse; KJV |
| Text and context | Exact verse wording and relevant surrounding passage |
| Direct observation | What the text says, without architectural labels |
| GUA-W placement(s) | Every supported place identified by authoritative GUA-W records |
| Placement evidence | Exact GUA-W source file and relevant section or wording |
| Contextual support | Why the verse supports the recorded relationship in context |
| Cross-placement | Additional supported places, each separately evidenced |
| Triple Constraint | Biblical compliance; scientific coherence where relevant; GUA-W consistency |
| Status | Placed / Multiple placements / Unresolved |
| Open question | What remains to be examined, if anything |

A verse is not complete merely because its reference appears in a list. It is accounted for when its context has been examined, its supported placements and evidence have been recorded, and any unresolved matter remains explicit.

## 7. Triple Constraint examination

- **Biblical compliance:** A placement must respect the verse's wording, grammar, literary setting, and canonical context. AI must not claim authority to determine meaning independently of Scripture.
- **Scientific coherence:** Where a proposed placement includes a claim about a created physical, biological, mathematical, or other scientific process, that claim must be examined using the relevant evidence and methods. A verse-to-architecture placement is not automatically a scientific mechanism or scientific confirmation.
- **GUA-W consistency:** A placement must agree with the completed GUA-W source records. The study may recognize and document; it may not add or alter the architecture.

A claim that fails any one constraint is not admitted as an accepted GUA-W placement.
