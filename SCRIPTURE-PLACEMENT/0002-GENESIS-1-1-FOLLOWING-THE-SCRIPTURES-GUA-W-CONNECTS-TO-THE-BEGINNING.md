# Scripture Placement Study 0002 — Genesis 1:1: Following the Scriptures GUA-W Connects to the Beginning

**Status:** In progress — root passage examined; three GUA-W-identified connected passages recorded  
**Created:** 2026-11-26  
**Research area:** Scripture / GUA-W-directed cross-reference  
**Series:** Separate Scripture Placement Study

## 1. Starting point

**Genesis 1:1 (KJV)**

> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.

This is the starting Scripture designated for this investigation. The task is not to ask AI to devise a chain of verses from its own interpretation. The task is to consult the completed GUA-W and ask which Scriptures its own records connect to this starting passage.

## 2. What the authoritative GUA-W says to examine next

The authoritative record **GUA-W-COMPLETED-ARCHITECTURE.md**, section 1, identifies John 1:1–3 and Hebrews 1:1–3 in its description of the Word and Creation. Section 2 also cites John 1:1–3, Colossians 1:16–17, and Hebrews 1:1–3 as canonical witnesses concerning Creation through the Word.

The authoritative record **GENESIS-1-THE-AUTHORIAL-ARCHITECTURE-AND-OPERATIVE-WORD.md**, sections 2–4, explicitly places these passages alongside Genesis 1:
- John 1:1–3
- Colossians 1:16–17
- Hebrews 1:1–3

Accordingly, the next passages GUA-W directs this study to examine are:

1. **John 1:1–3**
2. **Colossians 1:16–17**
3. **Hebrews 1:1–3**

These are source-directed connections, not passages selected by AI because it independently decided that they should fit.

## 3. Why these passages are connected

The GUA-W source records explicitly relate Genesis 1's account of God's creation to the scriptural testimony concerning the Word and the making and continuance of Creation through the Son.

The relationship recorded by GUA-W is:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

The study records that relationship as it appears in the authoritative source. It does not add a new layer, stage, Gear, State term, mechanism, or architectural definition.

The passages are not interchangeable:
- Genesis 1:1 is the starting statement in the Genesis account.
- John 1:1–3 speaks of the Word in the beginning and the making of all things through Him.
- Colossians 1:16–17 speaks of all things created by and for the Son, and of all things consisting in Him.
- Hebrews 1:1–3 speaks of God's speech by the Son, the making of the worlds through Him, and His upholding of all things.

Each passage must be read in its own context. The cross-reference does not erase the distinctions between the texts.

## 4. The next-step rule

For each of the three connected passages, the next examination asks the authoritative GUA-W records:

- What other Scripture references does GUA-W explicitly connect to this passage or to the relationship it witnesses?
- Does GUA-W identify more than one established placement?
- What source record and section supports each connection?
- What further Scripture does that source direct the investigation to examine?

The chain may branch. A passage can connect to several others, and several passages can connect to the same established GUA-W place. Preserve every supported connection. Do not force a single linear sequence where GUA-W gives multiple connections.

If an apparent connection comes only from AI's word association or speculation and is not supported by the authoritative GUA-W record, do not record it as a GUA-W-directed next passage. Keep it separate as an unverified question, if it warrants further investigation.

## 5. Triple Constraint examination

- **Biblical compliance:** The passages must be read in context and their distinct wording preserved.
- **Scientific coherence:** No scientific mechanism is asserted by this scriptural cross-reference. If a later investigation makes a scientific claim, it must be examined using the relevant scientific evidence and methods.
- **GUA-W consistency:** The three connected passages are explicitly identified in the completed GUA-W records. No architectural change is made.

## 6. Findings

1. Genesis 1:1 is the designated starting passage.
2. The authoritative GUA-W records explicitly connect the Genesis creation account with John 1:1–3, Colossians 1:16–17, and Hebrews 1:1–3.
3. These three passages are therefore the next passages to examine in this map-directed investigation.
4. The connections are recorded as a branch, not forced into a single next-verse chain.
5. The further passages connected to John, Colossians, and Hebrews have not yet been exhaustively retrieved from the authoritative GUA-W records. No next links beyond the three source-identified passages are claimed here.
6. This study records source-directed recognition; it does not claim that the whole Scripture corpus has been accounted for.
