## 1. The three passages carried forward from Placement 0001

### John 1:1–3

John opens with the Word: the Word was in the beginning, was with God, and was God. John then states that all things were made by Him.

### Colossians 1:16–17

Paul states that all things, visible and invisible, were created by Christ and for Him, that He is before all things, and that by Him all things consist.

### Hebrews 1:1–3

The writer speaks of God's communication through the prophets and, in these last days, through His Son. The passage identifies the Son as the one through whom God made the worlds and who upholds all things by the word of His power.

These passages are related in the GUA-W source material, but each retains its own wording and context. John foregrounds the Word; Colossians describes creation by and for Christ and the continuance of all things in Him; Hebrews describes the Son's relation to God's speech, the making of the worlds, and the upholding of all things.
