# Scripture Placement 0002 — The Word, the Son, and Creation

**Study:** Separate Scripture Placement  
**Record:** 0002  
**Passages examined:** John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–17; Hebrews 1:1–3 (KJV)

## Relationship to Placement 0001

Placement 0001 began with Genesis 1:1 and recorded the three companion passages explicitly identified in the relevant GUA-W Genesis 1 sources. This record examines those passages in their own wording and preserves their different emphases.

## 1. John 1:1–3 — the Word

John writes that the Word was in the beginning, was with God, and was God. He states that all things were made by Him and that without Him was not anything made that was made.

**Textual contribution:** The passage explicitly identifies the Word in relation to God and all created things. Its wording distinguishes the Word from the things made.

## 2. Colossians 1:16–17 — creation by and for Christ

Paul states that all things were created by Christ and for Him, including things visible and invisible. He is before all things, and by Him all things consist.

**Textual contribution:** The passage identifies Christ's relation to the whole created order and its continuance.

## 3. Hebrews 1:1–3 — the Son and the upholding of all things

The passage states that God has spoken by His Son, by whom also He made the worlds. It describes the Son as the brightness of God's glory and the express image of His person, and states that He upholds all things by the word of His power.

**Textual contribution:** The passage connects God's speech through the Son, the making of the worlds, and the Son's upholding of all things.
