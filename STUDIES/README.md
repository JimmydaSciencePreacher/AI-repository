## Repository method

This folder contains individual research investigations. Each study should identify its question, scope, sources, observations, analysis, findings, limitations, and unresolved questions, following [Guide 04 — Study Format](../GUIDES/04-STUDY-FORMAT.md).

Studies must distinguish source statements from interpretation and inference. A proposed GUA-W placement must be examined under the Triple Constraint Law. Unresolved questions must remain visible.

No study becomes established GUA-W architecture merely because it appears in this folder. Its evidential status and relationship to authoritative GUA-W records must be stated in the study itself.

## Continuity rule

Before continuing study writing:

1. Read the latest handoff in `HANDOFFS/`.
2. Read the immediately preceding study in full.
3. Inspect this index and the full `STUDIES/` directory.
4. Confirm the next unused study number.
5. Follow the Research Method and Study Format guides.
6. Do not create a duplicate study number, assume the next topic, or replace an existing study with a new draft.
7. Verify every created or changed file by fetching it back from GitHub.

A handoff is a historical record of work and continuity, not an authority to predetermine future conclusions.
