# Studies

## Study 0001

- [Reception, Information, Evidence, and Interpretation](0001-RECEPTION-INFORMATION-EVIDENCE-INTERPRETATION.md) — examines how a Witness receives and reports information, how information functions as evidence for a claim, and where interpretation and error enter.

This folder contains individual research investigations.

Each study should identify its question, scope, sources, observations, analysis, findings, limitations, and unresolved questions as appropriate. Use the structure in [Guide 04 — Study Format](../GUIDES/04-STUDY-FORMAT.md), adapting it to the investigation rather than filling headings mechanically.

Studies should distinguish source statements from interpretation and inference. A proposed GUA-W placement should be examined under the Triple Constraint Law. Unresolved questions should remain visible.

No study should be treated as established GUA-W architecture merely because it appears in this folder. Its evidential status and relationship to the reference material should be stated in the study itself.
