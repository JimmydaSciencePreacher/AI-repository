## 2. What we actually did with Genesis 2:25

Consider the sequence examined in Records 0064 and 0065.

Genesis 2:25 states:

> “And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed.”

Genesis 3:7 then records:

> “And the eyes of them both were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons.”

Genesis 3:10 records Adam's words:

> “I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked; and I hid myself.”

We did not remain at the surface of one verse.

We traveled through the Scriptural sequence:

1. **Backward to the prior condition:** Genesis 2:25 establishes that nakedness was already present before the transgression and that the man and woman were not ashamed.
2. **Forward into the changed recognition:** Genesis 3:7 records knowing and the making of coverings.
3. **Forward into the response:** Genesis 3:8–10 records hiding and Adam's stated fear.
4. **Further into the encounter:** Genesis 3:11–13 records God's questions and the humans' answers concerning the command and transgression.
5. **Across the canonical record:** other passages can be examined to clarify the larger relationship, while the immediate Genesis wording remains intact.

Then we used the completed GUA-W as the map through which to examine the relationship between the condition, recognition, and expression.

The map helped us ask what was present, what was later registered, and what response followed. Returning to the Scripture then tested whether the mapping preserved the actual text.

This was not a one-way exercise in attaching a GUA-W label to a verse. It was reciprocal investigation: Scripture gave us the observed sequence; GUA-W helped us navigate its relationships; Scripture remained the test of what we claimed to have seen.

## 3. Two directions of travel

The completed architecture has an established order:

**Ontic Necessity → Distinction → Admissibility → Constraint → Structure → Framework → Pattern → Reception → Activation → Differentiation → Propagation → Integration → Expression → Witnessed realized configuration.**

The State layer describes the realized configuration and its witness-state; Identity, Alignment, Persistence, Witness-state, and Realized configuration are not additional sequential runtime stages. Drift is not an admissible State condition. These distinctions must be preserved exactly as the completed architecture states them.

The investigator can travel the map in two complementary directions.

### A. Backward investigation — from witnessed expression toward foundations

Begin with an observed or reported condition: a result, event, structure, behavior, measurement, or expression.

Then ask:

- What exactly is being witnessed?
- What is its realized configuration?
- What relationships and operations are evident in the expression?
- What framework and pattern are involved, if the evidence supports identifying them?
- What distinctions, constraints, and admissibility conditions govern the relationship?
- What foundational realities must be preserved to understand the observation correctly?

This is a backward investigative trace. It seeks dependencies, conditions, boundaries, and foundations that help explain the witnessed expression.

It does **not** claim that Creation runs backward. It does **not** reverse the Author's creative order. It is the investigator tracing from an encountered result toward the relationships and foundations relevant to understanding it.

### B. Forward investigation — from foundations toward witnessed expression

Begin with an established Scriptural statement, a well-supported foundational condition, or a clearly identified constraint.

Then ask:

- What distinctions must be preserved?
- What is admissible under the governing conditions?
- What structure is recognized in the completed map?
- What framework and pattern are relevant?
- How do the established runtime relationships proceed?
- What expression or realized configuration is actually witnessed?

This is a forward investigative trace. It follows the map from foundations toward expression, testing each proposed relationship against the evidence.

It does **not** authorize us to predict a particular physical outcome without evidence. The map guides the questions; the relevant discipline's methods establish what can be said about its domain.

### C. Why both directions matter

Backward travel without forward examination can stop at a plausible explanation without testing its consequences or correspondence with the source.

Forward travel without backward examination can attach a foundational label to an outcome without understanding the conditions and dependencies that produced the observation.

The two directions correct one another.

**Backward: What must be understood to account for what is witnessed?**

**Forward: What does the established order lead us to examine, and what is actually expressed?**

The map is navigable in both directions as an investigative instrument, while its established architecture and the Author's order remain unchanged.

## 5. The same method across the sciences

This is the methodological bridge to cross-disciplinary investigation.

A scientific discipline encounters a particular domain of the One Creation. It has its own questions, terms, evidence, measurements, methods, models, and standards. Those must remain intact.

The investigator can then use GUA-W as the common map—not to make the disciplines identical, but to trace relationships among their findings.

For example, a research question may begin with a measured physical expression. The investigator first establishes exactly what the relevant source measured and what its result supports. From that expression, the investigator can travel backward through supported dependencies and constraints. The investigator can then travel forward through the relevant established relationships and ask what further expressions or observations should be examined.

A second discipline may investigate a different part of the same created order. Its evidence is independently established according to its proper methods. The map then provides a route for asking whether the two findings share a dependency, boundary, constraint, relationship, or larger order.

The connection is not accepted because two subjects use similar words. It is investigated because the evidence indicates a relationship worth tracing.

### The cross-disciplinary route

1. **Enter through the discipline:** identify its question and source evidence.
2. **Establish the finding:** determine what was measured, observed, derived, or modeled, and preserve its scope.
3. **Travel backward:** trace supported dependencies, conditions, constraints, and relevant foundations.
4. **Navigate the completed GUA-W:** identify the established relationships and distinctions that may locate the finding within the unified map.
5. **Travel forward:** examine how those relationships connect to further operations or expressions, without predicting beyond the evidence.
6. **Enter the neighboring discipline:** establish its findings independently and use the map to examine the possible connection.
7. **Return to both sources:** verify that the proposed relationship preserves each discipline's actual evidence and methods.
8. **Apply the Triple Constraint Law:** test each material claim for Biblical compliance, Scientific coherence, and GUA-W consistency.
9. **Record the recognition:** state the relationship, its precise placement where supported, the evidence, limitations, counterevidence, and unresolved questions.

This method does not make physics biology, biology theology, or mathematics any other discipline. Their subjects and methods remain differentiated. The unity being investigated is the unity of the Creation they examine.
